## Overview

Practical reconnaissance work focused on turning an unknown target into a structured attack surface that can be investigated methodically.

## Techniques

- Service and endpoint discovery
- Directory and content enumeration
- Technology fingerprinting
- HTTP response analysis
- Interesting-file and backup-file discovery
- Prioritization of exposed functionality

## Tooling

Nmap, Dirsearch, Burp Suite, browser developer tools, Linux command-line utilities, and small scripts for repeatable tasks.

## Methodology

The objective is not simply to find more endpoints, but to identify the parts of an application that are most likely to contain security-relevant behaviour and then validate those observations carefully.
