## Workflow

My security practice follows a repeatable loop:

1. Scope the target and confirm authorization.
2. Enumerate the application and its attack surface.
3. Form hypotheses from observed behaviour.
4. Test one control at a time.
5. Capture concise evidence.
6. Assess impact and likely root cause.
7. Document remediation and lessons learned.
8. Convert useful findings into reusable notes.

## Why this matters

A useful security assessment is more than finding an interesting response. I aim to produce evidence that another tester can understand, reproduce, and use to improve the application.
